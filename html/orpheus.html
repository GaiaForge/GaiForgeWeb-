Orpheus

Autonomous Bioacoustic Playback System

Solar-Powered Audio Lure. Autonomous. Built for Wildlife Research.

Overview

Orpheus is a sophisticated bioacoustic playback system designed for ecosystem management, habitat restoration, and wildlife research. Deployed as an autonomous acoustic audio lure at bird ringing stations, ecoacoustic monitoring sites, and conservation reserves worldwide, Orpheus gives researchers precise control over sound playback based on astronomical events, time schedules, and seasonal patterns — all while running on solar power for months without intervention. Available in Basic and Pro versions.

Not sure which approach fits your study design? Read our guide to bioacoustic field equipment — passive monitoring vs active playback, and where Orpheus fits.

Each Orpheus unit is hand-built to order. Pricing and lead times vary based on your configuration and current order queue. Contact us to discuss your needs and get a quote.

4 Intelligent Playback Modes

Orpheus Basic and Pro both feature four powerful playback modes designed for research-grade precision:

Manual Playback

Direct audio control with Play, Pause, and Stop functionality. Perfect for on-demand playback during field observations or controlled experiments.

Interval Playback

Schedule audio at precise time windows throughout the day. Set specific intervals for targeted playback aligned with your research protocol.

Astral Playback

Sun-synchronized scheduling that automatically adjusts to sunrise and sunset times. Ideal for studying crepuscular species and dawn/dusk behaviors.

Seasonal Playback

Natural season-based scheduling (Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter) with support for custom date ranges. Perfect for migration studies and breeding seasons.

Key Features

  • Astronomical-Based Playback: Schedule bioacoustic playback relative to sunrise and sunset, automatically adjusting to seasonal light changes at any deployment location worldwide
  • Seasonal Programming: Define custom seasons for migration studies or use natural seasons with specific audio schedules for each
  • Location-Aware Scheduling: Latitude/longitude configuration ensures accurate sun calculations for any deployment location on Earth
  • Solar Power Integration: Built-in power management with real-time solar monitoring and deep sleep capabilities
  • Companion App Control: Configure and monitor remotely via Bluetooth with the Orpheus mobile app
  • Environmental Monitoring (Pro): Integrated sensors track temperature, humidity, and barometric pressure with data logging
  • High-Quality Recording (Pro): Capture animal responses with configurable bit depth (16/24/32-bit float) and sample rates
  • Comprehensive Logging (Pro): Detailed playback analytics with timestamps and location data for research documentation
  • AI Species Identification (Pro): Automatically identifies detected species from recordings, visualized in the research portal
  • Automated PDF Reports (Pro): Generate and export research-ready reports directly from your portal dashboard
  • Multi-Device Management (Pro): Monitor and manage multiple Orpheus deployments from a single portal account

Technical Specifications

  • Processor: Custom integrated single-board computer with proprietary firmware
  • Power: Solar-powered with dedicated low-power management controller and scheduled wake
  • Current Draw: Optimized to ~50mA (67% reduction from initial design)
  • Sensors (Pro): Environmental (temperature, humidity, pressure) and power monitoring
  • Audio Output: High-quality amplified speaker system
  • Recording (Pro): USB audio device support, 16/24/32-bit float, configurable sample rates
  • Connectivity: Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) for remote app control
  • Display: Touchscreen interface with real-time status and controls
  • Storage: Expandable storage for large audio libraries
  • Enclosure: Weatherproof design for field deployment
  • GPIO: Integration for external equipment control (relays, triggers)
