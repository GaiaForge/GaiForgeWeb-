HiveGuard

Bioacoustic Beehive Intelligence

Know what's happening inside your hives — without opening them.

In Development

Development Status: HiveGuard is in active development and field testing. The hardware, firmware, and cloud platform are functional. Check back for updates on availability.

Overview

HiveGuard listens to your bees. By analysing the acoustic signature of a colony in real time, it can tell you whether the queen is present, if swarming is imminent, or if the colony is under stress — all without disturbing the hive. Combined with environmental sensors and hive weight tracking, HiveGuard gives beekeepers a complete picture of colony health from their phone.

Data syncs to the cloud for long-term trend analysis across your entire apiary. Designed for the field — weatherproof, battery-powered, and built to run for months on a set of AA batteries.

Features

  • Colony Sound Analysis: Continuously monitors the acoustic signature of the hive to detect changes in colony behaviour and mood
  • Queen Detection: Identifies queen piping and queen presence from the hive's sound profile — know immediately if your queen is lost
  • Swarm Prediction: Detects pre-swarm acoustic patterns days before the colony leaves, giving you time to intervene
  • Stress & Defence Alerts: Recognises agitated or stressed colonies from sound alone — pesticide exposure, predators, disease
  • Smart Alerts: Only notifies you when it's confident — no false alarms from wind, rain, or passing traffic
  • Environmental Monitoring: Tracks temperature, humidity, and barometric pressure inside and around the hive
  • Hive Weight: Dual load cells detect nectar flows, consumption patterns, and sudden weight drops that indicate swarming
  • Mobile App: Live dashboard on your phone via Bluetooth — see colony status, adjust settings, and retrieve data
  • Cloud Dashboard: Monitor all your hives from anywhere with health scores, trends, and historical analysis
  • Long Battery Life: Runs for months on standard AA batteries. Solar-ready for permanent installations

How It Works

Listen

A sensitive microphone inside the hive captures the colony's sound signature throughout the day

Understand

On-device analysis identifies what the bees are telling you — queen status, stress, swarming intent, or healthy activity

Alert

Get notified on your phone when something needs attention. Walk up to the hive and see live data via Bluetooth

Track

Data syncs to the cloud so you can see trends over weeks and months — catch problems before they become emergencies

What HiveGuard Can Tell You

Queen Present

Confirms your queen is active and the colony is queenright — the foundation of a healthy hive

Swarm Warning

Detects pre-swarm behaviour days in advance, giving you time to split the hive or take preventive action

Queen Lost

Alerts you when the colony shows signs of queenlessness — critical for timely requeening

Colony Stress

Identifies stressed or agitated colonies that may be dealing with disease, pests, or environmental threats

Nectar Flow

Weight tracking shows when foragers are bringing in nectar — know the best time to add supers

Environmental Conditions

Temperature and humidity trends help you understand how weather affects your colonies

What Makes HiveGuard Different

Most hive monitors on the market measure temperature and weight. HiveGuard goes further — it actually listens to your bees and understands what they're saying.

Sound-First Intelligence

Other monitors tell you it's getting warmer. HiveGuard tells you the queen is missing. Acoustic analysis reveals colony behaviour that temperature and weight alone cannot

On-Device Processing

No cloud dependency for real-time alerts. HiveGuard analyses sound locally and alerts you immediately — even with no internet connection

Truly Field-Ready

Months of battery life on standard AA cells. No solar panel required, no wiring, no subscriptions. Place it on the hive and forget about it

Your Data, Your Control

All data is stored locally on the device. Cloud sync is optional and free — no subscription fees, no data lock-in, no third-party access

No False Alarms

Smart confidence scoring means you only get notified when something genuinely needs attention — not every time the wind blows

Affordable Monitoring

Enterprise-level colony intelligence at a fraction of the cost of competing systems. No recurring subscription fees
